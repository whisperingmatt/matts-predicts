### Caveats that travel with these tables

Sector, exchange, and scalemarketcap are the tickers table's current values, not point-in-time; scalemarketcap in particular records size after any move and its ratios (0.34 for Small, 1.75 for Mega on launch_300) describe where winners ended up. The point-in-time size fields are market cap and its decile. The 5.02 proxy for CEO change also counts director elections and compensation arrangements and fires for over 80% of rows a year. Insider fields start in 2009 and 13F fields in 2013, which the coverage columns show. The T-6 and T-12 decile columns are the GROWTH-002 lag-6 and lag-12 ranks of the same rows. The 8-K counts distinguish almost nothing before T-0; what separates winners is price history, size, volatility, and profitability, and during the move it is equity issuance.
