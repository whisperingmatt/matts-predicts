### Section 3.5 — win_100 against loss_50

The base over 558,318 pooled rows is a win_100 rate of 3.65% against a loss_50 rate of 7.59%, a ratio of 0.48, with a median 12-month return of +5.5%. Across the 23 features and 222 decile cells at T-0 (221 D8-sufficient), the ratio sits between 0.30 and 0.82 in all but two cells, with a median of 0.52. The two rates move together: every decile that raises the win_100 rate raises the loss_50 rate by more. The cells with the highest win rates are also the cells with the highest loss rates and the lowest median returns. Realized volatility decile 10 has a win rate of 9.5% and a loss rate of 22.8% (ratio 0.42, median −9.4%); distance from the 52-week high decile 10 has 8.7% and 21.0% (0.41, −7.0%); market cap decile 1 has 6.8% and 17.8% (0.38, −3.9%); share issuance decile 10 has 5.6% and 16.6% (0.34, −1.9%); shareholder yield decile 1 has 5.8% and 16.9% (0.34, −2.5%); 13F ownership decile 1 has 5.0% and 14.6% (0.34, 0.0%). The free-cash-flow slope is lopsided at both ends (0.30 at decile 1, 0.34 at decile 10), so extreme FCF change in either direction goes with losses more than with wins. Six-month momentum is U-shaped in both rates: decile 1 wins 7.4% and loses 17.2% (0.43), decile 10 wins 6.1% and loses 12.1% (0.50), and deciles 4 to 8 sit at 0.48 to 0.51 with median returns near +7.6%.

The cells with the best balance are quiet ones. 13F ownership deciles 4 to 8 run 0.60 to 0.80 with median returns of +6.6% to +7.9%, against 0.34 at decile 1 and 0.50 at decile 10. Dividend yield decile 5 (11,744 rows, both rates 2.3%, ratio 0.97, median +11.0%) and market cap decile 10 (1.1% against 2.5%, ratio 0.43, median +10.1%) have the highest median returns among large cells, with low win rates. The single cell above 1.0, insider buyers decile 7 at 1.69 (11.3% against 6.7%, median +23.9%), is a regime artifact: its 5,564 rows come from 15 months (April to December 2008, May 2009, August to October 2011, March to May 2020) in which broad insider buying pushed one-buyer rows out of decile 1; it marks market troughs, not a property of the stocks. The only insufficient cell at T-0 is realized volatility decile 1 (94 wins).

### Caveats that travel with these tables

Sector, exchange, and scalemarketcap are the tickers table's current values, not point-in-time; scalemarketcap in particular records size after any move and its ratios (0.34 for Small, 1.75 for Mega on launch_300) describe where winners ended up. The point-in-time size fields are market cap and its decile. The 5.02 proxy for CEO change also counts director elections and compensation arrangements and fires for over 80% of rows a year. Insider fields start in 2009 and 13F fields in 2013, which the coverage columns show. The T-6 and T-12 decile columns are the GROWTH-002 lag-6 and lag-12 ranks of the same rows. The 8-K counts distinguish almost nothing before T-0; what separates winners is price history, size, volatility, and profitability, and during the move it is equity issuance.
